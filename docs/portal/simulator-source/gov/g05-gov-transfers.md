# USD across banks to a local SGD account

A corporate treasury funds its own SGD account at a third bank in Singapore from a US dollar account in the US.

DBS documents the bilateral USD leg. The FX and third-bank leg are model assumptions, as is AFC participation.

Baseline: https://www.dbs.com/newsroom/DBS_and_Kinexys_by_JP_Morgan_launch_industry_first_247_interoperability_solution_for_cross_border_tokenised_deposit_transfers

Completion evidence: SGD credited at the third bank
